## $${\color{red}AEO \ Cheat \ Sheet}$$

### What is AEO?

**Answer Engine Optimization** is the practice of structuring content so **answer engines** (Google SGE, Bing Copilot, ChatGPT, Perplexity, voice assistants) can **directly extract and present clear answers** to user questions.

> SEO = ranking pages
> **AEO = being the answer**

---

## 🎯 Core Goal

Deliver **precise, authoritative, machine-readable answers** to specific questions.

---

## 🔍 Where AEO Matters

* Google Featured Snippets
* Google SGE (AI Overviews)
* Bing Copilot
* ChatGPT / Perplexity citations
* Voice search (Alexa, Siri, Google Assistant)
* In-app search & AI tools

---

## 🧩 AEO vs SEO (Quick Compare)

| SEO               | AEO                   |
| ----------------- | --------------------- |
| Keywords          | Questions & intents   |
| Long-form content | Direct answers        |
| Ranking focus     | Answer extraction     |
| CTR-driven        | Zero-click visibility |
| Page authority    | Entity authority      |

---

## ❓ Question Types to Target

* **What is…**
* **How does…**
* **Why does…**
* **When should…**
* **Best way to…**
* **Difference between A and B**
* **Pros and cons of…**

👉 One question = one clear answer block

---

## 🧱 Ideal AEO Answer Structure

```
Question (H2/H3)
Direct Answer (40–60 words)
Supporting Details (bullets)
Example (optional)
```

✅ Answer should make sense **without reading the rest of the page**

---

## ✍️ Writing Rules for AEO

* Answer first, explain later
* Use **simple, declarative sentences**
* Avoid fluff, metaphors, intros
* Define acronyms immediately
* Use **lists, tables, steps**
* One idea per paragraph
* Present facts, not marketing

---

## 🧠 Content Formats That Win

* FAQs
* How-to guides
* Definitions
* Comparisons
* Step-by-step instructions
* Checklists
* Cheat sheets (👋 this one)

---

## 🧾 Schema & Structured Data (VERY Important)

Use:

* `FAQPage`
* `HowTo`
* `Article`
* `Breadcrumb`
* `Product`
* `Organization`
* `Person`

📌 Helps AI **understand + trust** your content

---

## 🔗 Entity Optimization

* Use **consistent entity names**
* Mention:

  * Brand
  * Product
  * Author
  * Location
* Link to authoritative sources (Wikipedia, docs, standards)

AI engines think in **entities**, not keywords.

---

## 🧪 AEO-Friendly HTML Tips

* Proper heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)
* `<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<table>` over long paragraphs
* Descriptive anchor text
* Fast loading, mobile-friendly

---

## 🎤 Voice Search Optimization

* Conversational phrasing
* Natural language questions
* Short answers (20–30 words)
* Local modifiers (“near me”, city names)

---

## 📏 Ideal Answer Lengths

* Definitions: **20–40 words**
* Explanations: **40–60 words**
* Steps: **3–7 steps**
* Lists: **5–9 items**

---

## 📊 How to Measure AEO Success

* Featured snippet ownership
* AI citation mentions
* Zero-click impressions
* Visibility in SGE / Bing Copilot
* Voice search responses

(Traditional CTR matters less)

---

## 🚀 AEO Optimization Checklist

✅ Clear questions in headings
✅ Direct answers immediately below
✅ Structured data implemented
✅ Entity consistency
✅ Author credibility (E-E-A-T)
✅ Updated content
✅ Fast, clean HTML

---

## 🧠 Pro Tip

> If your content can be copy-pasted as an answer **without editing**, you’re doing AEO right.
